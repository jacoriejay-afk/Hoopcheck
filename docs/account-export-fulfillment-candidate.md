# Durable account export candidate — owned-records-v1

This is a review-only database candidate. It is not a migration, a legal fulfillment claim, or enabled infrastructure. The private policy row starts disabled; no cron or provider is activated. The scope is explicitly incomplete and responses always declare complete:false. Owner and worker wrappers expose only narrow RPCs; no raw table grants. The owner requires the existing live-session and MFA proofs, confirmed email, non-anonymous non-OAuth authenticated identity and allowed moderation status. Job identifiers are idempotency keys, not owner selectors. One active job per owner. Worker transaction uses SKIP LOCKED and atomic materialization; interrupted transactions roll back. Retry count/delay and download expiry have no invented legal defaults. Cancellation and expiration scrub data; audit events contain controlled codes only. Downloads use stable ordinal pagination, with no signed URL or public object.

Projected sections (27): profile, reviews, womens_reviews, player_posts, fan_posts, feed_comments, fan_comments, review_comments, sent_messages, outgoing_contact_requests, group_posts, group_memberships, owned_groups, club_group_memberships, club_group_posts, club_group_comments, subscriptions, notification_metadata, privacy_requests, support_requests, support_messages, career_history, bookmarks, checks, poll_votes, posting_activity, live_chat_messages.

Still excluded pending reviewed schema/field and disclosure scope: received messages, third-party identifiers/details, original media bytes and provider assets, device/session and credential records, raw billing provider payloads and receipts, membership-source tables absent from reviewed baseline, internal moderation/security data, other tables not explicitly projected. User-entered text may itself contain third-party information; requires disclosure review. Audit metadata retention and legal holds, scheduling registration, UI integration, high-volume load limits, actual schema and role acceptance, and independent policy approval remain required. Never claim this completes a privacy request. No hosted execution.
