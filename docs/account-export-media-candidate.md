# Original media export candidate

This extends the existing owned-records-v1 durable export; it does not replace or reclassify the 27 record categories. A separate default-off media policy and explicit per-bucket and per-object decisions control disclosure. Source ownership is checked against the reviewed storage catalog. Only a current, non-archived, non-versioned exact object version is eligible. The inventory is immutable per job and remains private; the owner manifest exposes opaque IDs and sizes only. Approved files are delivered through a separately verified server endpoint, not public/signed object URLs. Cancellation, failure or expiry removes the entire inventory. No original filename, path, raw storage metadata, credential, third-party media, or unrelated record is returned. Original bytes may themselves contain third-party information; approval must confirm original disclosure rather than assume redaction. The 20 MiB limit is a conservative technical bound, not a legal rule; larger and external provider files, ownerless legacy objects, versioned storage, redaction tools and durable staging remain unresolved. A changing object becomes unavailable rather than substituting another file. Full legal fulfillment stays incomplete. No hosted registration or execution.
