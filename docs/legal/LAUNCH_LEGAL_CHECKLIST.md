# HoopCheck — Legal Launch Checklist

> Founder working document. This is not legal advice and should be reviewed by a U.S. startup/business attorney before launch.

## 1. Company
- Choose the state/country of formation with counsel.
- For a Florida launch, Sunbiz currently lists $100 for Articles of Organization plus a $25 registered-agent designation fee for a new LLC. Confirm current fees before filing.
- File the entity.
- Obtain EIN.
- Open a dedicated business bank account.
- Keep company and personal money separate.
- Sign an operating agreement.
- Record 100% founder ownership at formation unless a lawyer documents another arrangement.
- Maintain a cap table and written ownership records.

## 2. Name and intellectual property
- Run a comprehensive trademark clearance search for HOOPCHECK and confusingly similar marks.
- Search USPTO, state databases, common-law/web uses, app stores, social platforms and relevant international markets.
- File the word mark before major marketing if counsel confirms clearance.
- Consider a separate logo/design mark.
- Assign all code, designs, databases, documentation and brand assets to the company.
- Register copyrights where strategically useful.
- Keep invention/IP assignment records.

## 3. Domain and brand
- Control the primary domain through a company-owned registrar account.
- Enable registrar MFA and domain lock.
- Register close defensive domains if practical.
- Put domain renewal on auto-renewal.
- Never let a contractor personally own the primary domain.

## 4. User-generated content / defamation
- Terms of Service must define acceptable reviews and user content.
- Reviews must be based on genuine first-hand experience.
- Separate opinions from factual allegations.
- Prohibit knowingly false statements, harassment, threats, extortion, doxxing, private information and review manipulation.
- Keep every review pending until moderation approval.
- Automatically flag high-risk allegation language for human review.
- Maintain a moderation audit trail.
- Provide report and appeal/correction channels.
- Preserve relevant evidence when a legal hold exists.
- Do not promise immunity from lawsuits or liability.
- Have counsel review the review/defamation workflow before launch.

## 5. Privacy / data
- Maintain Privacy Policy, Terms, Community Guidelines and data-retention rules.
- Document what personal data is collected and why.
- Minimize sensitive data.
- Define retention/deletion schedules.
- Establish an account-deletion process.
- Establish a privacy-request process.
- Review U.S. state privacy laws and international laws that may apply to HoopCheck users.
- Use DPAs/security terms with vendors that process user data.
- Review Stripe, Supabase, hosting, identity-verification and email-provider terms.

## 6. Security
- MFA for Founder/admin/GitHub/Supabase/Stripe/Vercel/email.
- Never expose secret/service-role keys.
- Least-privilege admin access.
- RLS on every exposed Supabase table.
- Strong password and session controls.
- Security headers and HTTPS.
- Dependency updates and vulnerability review.
- Backups and restore testing.
- Incident-response plan.
- Breach-notification decision tree.
- Vendor-security review.
- Cyber insurance quote.

## 7. Contracts
Maintain company-approved templates for:
- Founder/operating agreement
- Contractor agreement
- IP assignment
- Confidentiality/NDA
- Data-processing/security addendum
- Team/league partnership agreement
- Sponsorship agreement
- Influencer/ambassador agreement
- Employment agreement when hiring employees

## 8. Insurance
Ask a broker about:
- General liability
- Technology errors & omissions / professional liability
- Cyber liability
- Media liability / defamation coverage
- Directors & officers coverage if/when appropriate
- Workers' compensation when required

## 9. Founder operating rules
- The company owns the code.
- The company owns the database and business data, subject to user rights and applicable law.
- User content remains subject to the license in the Terms.
- No contractor gets permanent ownership merely because they wrote code unless intentionally granted in a signed agreement.
- Keep a signed record of every equity grant.
- Keep a record of every material vendor and data processor.

## 10. Launch gate
Do not call HoopCheck legally launch-ready until counsel has reviewed:
1. Entity/ownership structure.
2. Trademark clearance.
3. Terms.
4. Privacy Policy.
5. Community Guidelines.
6. Review/defamation moderation policy.
7. Contractor/IP agreements.
8. Data/security practices.
9. Insurance.
10. Payment/subscription/refund terms.
