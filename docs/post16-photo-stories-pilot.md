# Photo Stories pilot — server boundary
Depends on expiring-content safety PR #1131. The server flag HOOPCHECK_PHOTO_STORIES_ENABLED defaults off. No production flag change or migration authorized. Before activation verify full deployed feed_posts schema accepts post_kind=story and community_visible=false, and actual effective SELECT policies enforce membership, both-direction blocks, follower-only audience and expiry (including every share chain). Service role must not be used to read a Story. Photo-only single moderated attachment; server-generated 24-hour expiry; no client expiry, location, tagging, broad audience, or video. Existing permanent posts remain permanent. Expiring content cannot be permanently reshared or sent to persistent inbox; short-lived signed media links are capped by expiration. Existing rows and media are not physically erased merely by expiry; storage lifecycle and retention policy must be rehearsed before public activation. Native photo Stories composer/viewer is implemented behind the default-off flag, including verified-session reads, follower-scope filtering and native file-backed multipart compatibility. Signed-device testing, old-client compatibility against effective schema, video-content moderation, and wider audience controls remain open. No hosted database writes, production changes or new builds.

Community photo Story candidate: separate server/native flags default off. The review-only policy must be independently audited and rehearsed on the reviewed baseline before enabling; eligibility, both-direction blocks, no anonymous access, expiry, and no resharing remain mandatory. Video remains blocked until actual visual/audio moderation exists.

Video draft preparation uses the existing native gallery/camera validation and local playback. A draft stays in the mounted account-scoped composer and is not persisted or uploaded by HoopCheck. Server publication and media signing refuse video Story attachments, even through reshares. Photo publication is unchanged. Before video upload or activation: review private quarantine and asset ownership, durable asset-bound visual AND audio moderation decisions, timeout/retry/cleanup, expiry, and web/native actual-device playback. Caption-only moderation does not qualify. No provider activation or public rollout.
