# Review retention candidate (QA only)

Install in disposable PG17 only, after worker/freeze and review-author ACL. Both author foreign keys change from CASCADE to RESTRICT and author_id can be null only through a fixed reviewed anonymization transition. The original destructive cleanup candidate is not a substitute for this path. Do not activate either path until the complete policy and dependency plan are independently approved.

The service-only worker RPC is disabled by default. It requires the exact fenced step-8 request, frozen owner, enabled policy and a reviewed digest of current rows plus verified status. An independent database operator must approve the scope, legal-hold clearance, external/historical linkage clearance and retention end date. No approval is seeded. The transition is atomic: original author and review identifiers, body, title and season are removed; IDs are rotated, dates are month-bucketed, ratings and existing target remain. No old-to-new ID map or original author is stored in the retained trust facts. Retry returns a count-only receipt. Both active-author uniqueness and row count are preserved; women's duplicates fail preflight instead of being silently deleted. Retained rows permit only existing moderation status changes, not rating/identity/text edits. Unresolved comments, likes, reports, moderation events, risk flags or unexpected dependencies block the whole batch. In particular, this is not yet a solution for review histories with moderation dependencies. No claims of irreversible anonymity are made: rare rating/target combinations, already downloaded content, provider copies, backups, historic exports, moderation snapshots and timing correlations require privacy review. Active-client account ownership still requires existing ACL and RLS.

Trust Score continues to count retained approved ratings; a service-only fact RPC supplies verification state without an author link. Month bucketing deliberately changes the recency component; exact score preservation and disclosure-risk tradeoffs require approval. Re-registration cannot be linked to a former identity without keeping identifying information. Retention dates and legal holds are stored, but the independent expiry/hold enforcement and scheduler are not yet implemented. Old candidate cleanup, exports and client compatibility require actual-schema rehearsal and combined acceptance. No provider or worker activation.

Synthetic regression is not the existing 57-assertion actual-schema runner.
